- Symbols: QQQ, SPY, IWM, DIA, NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, META, TSLA
- Tune 2020-07-27 to 2025-10-24 (1319 days); check 2025-10-27 to 2026-10-26 (251 days)
- Model: first 5-minute candle sets the direction, 5-min trend and VWAP must agree, market entry 9:36, stop beyond the opening range (x the stop width), hold to the close or 10R
- 'per week' counts trades across all symbols. Net R = after the cost of trading it that way:
  - deep ITM monthly: $0.10 spread, 0.004% of price/hour decay, delta 0.8
  - weekly ATM: $0.05 spread, 0.011% of price/hour decay, delta 0.5
  - 1-2 DTE ATM: $0.03 spread, 0.030% of price/hour decay, delta 0.45
  - shares: $0.01 spread, 0.000% of price/hour decay, delta 1.0
